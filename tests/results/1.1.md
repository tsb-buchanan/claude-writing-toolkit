## Open points

1. The 1.0 open points still hold.
2. Without a map, human-write still sometimes renames "totes" to "crates" (RP04), as in 1.0. It says so in its lists, so the writer sees it.
3. In Claude Code, human-write reads only its section, so on a Word file it cannot see that a paragraph repeats one in another section (RP05). On claude.ai the whole file is in the chat.
4. A metaphor can become a literal claim that the text implies but does not state, such as "idle most of the time". Every run listed it as a meaning shift.
5. Several runs changed "would be higher" to "will be higher" in the 200-use limit, and listed it. The limit stayed.
6. doc-check quick on the thesis still sometimes misreads the contribution statement.
