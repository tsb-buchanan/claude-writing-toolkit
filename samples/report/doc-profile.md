# Writing profile
toolkit: 1.0

## Document

mode: report
audience: the Operations Board of Tidewell Grocers; busy decision makers, and many read only page one
key_message: Roll out reusable crates to all four depots from April, because they cut the packaging cost per order by 31%. The board is asked to approve $304,000.
opening: the first paragraph states the message and the decision needed
parts:
1. Summary
2. Background
3. How the pilot worked
4. Results
5. Risks and costs of a full rollout
6. Recommendation and next steps
length: the summary fits on one page
keep:
1. Direct quotations.
2. Contract and legal text.
budget: auto

## Style guide

1. Voice: "we" for Tidewell Grocers. Name other teams and suppliers for their work.
2. Spelling: US English.
3. Sentences: at most 25 words. A longer sentence needs a reason.
4. Words to cut: leverage, going forward, in order to, very, basically, it is important to note that.
5. Dashes: no em dashes. Write ranges as "5 to 10".
6. Numbers: one to nine in words, digits from 10. Use a thousands separator and the % sign.
7. House rules: none.
