## Style guide

1. Voice: "I". Name my supervisor and co-authors for their work.
2. Spelling: UK English.
3. Sentences: at most 25 words. A longer sentence needs a reason.
4. Words to cut: very, clearly, obviously, it is worth noting, novel.
5. Dashes: no em dashes. Write ranges as "5 to 10", also in LaTeX: no "--" in prose.
6. Numbers: digits with units (3 minutes, 20 returns per hour). Counts from one to nine in words.
7. House rules: No equations in a chapter's first section.
